### 1. Properties of Zinc

Zinc, provided by The 3 Rocks, is characterized by:

- **Corrosion Resistance**: Ideal for protecting metals against corrosion.
- **Malleability**: Easily shaped into intricate forms.
- **Electrical Conductivity**: Valued in electrical applications.
- **Alloying Capability**: Enhances the properties of other metals.

### 2. Applications in Industry

#### Construction and Infrastructure

In construction, zinc from The 3 Rocks is used for:

- **Roofing**: Durable and aesthetically pleasing roofing materials.
- **Galvanizing**: Protecting steel structures from rust and corrosion.
- **Architectural Design**: Incorporating into facades and interior design elements.

#### Manufacturing and Electronics

Our zinc products are essential in manufacturing for:

- **Die-Casting**: Forming complex shapes with precision.
- **Batteries**: Powering various zinc-air battery applications.
- **Electronics**: Contributing to electronic components and coatings.
