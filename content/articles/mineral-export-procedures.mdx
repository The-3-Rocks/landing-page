### Key Export Steps

#### 1. Mining Directorate Authorization
All mineral exports must be approved by the Ministry of Energy Transition and Sustainable Development. This ensures the material was legally mined and royalties have been paid.

#### 2. CADEX (Office des Changes)
Foreign currency transactions are regulated. We ensure all financial documentation complies with Moroccan exchange control regulations to prevent payment delays.

#### 3. Port Logistics
We utilize Morocco's major ports for bulk and container shipments:
- **Casablanca**: Primary hub for containerized mineral exports.
- **Jorf Lasfar**: Specialized bulk terminal.
- **Tangier Med**: World-class connectivity for rapid transit to Europe.

#### 4. Incoterms
We typically offer **FOB (Free On Board)** or **CIF (Cost, Insurance, and Freight)** terms, organizing transport capabilities from the mine gate to your destination port.

### Efficiency in Motion

Our experienced logistics team ensures that documentation is prepared in parallel with mining, minimizing dwell time at the port and getting materials to your factory faster.
