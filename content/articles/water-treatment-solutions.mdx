### Treatment Applications

#### Copper Sulfate
- **Algae Control**: Highly effective in reservoirs and irrigation canals to prevent algal blooms that can clog pipes and affect taste.

#### Filter Media
- **Anthracite**: Used in dual-media filtration to remove turbidity.
- **Barite**: High-density media for specific separation processes.

#### Chemical Precipitation
- **Iron Salts**: Ferric chloride (derived from iron) is a common coagulant used to remove suspended solids and phosphates from wastewater.
