### Critical Electronic Materials

#### Copper: The Universal Conductor
- **Printed Circuit Boards (PCBs)**: Copper foil is the conductive pathway for all electronics.
- **Wiring**: Essential for power and data transmission.
- **Heat Sinks**: Thermal management in high-performance processors.

#### Antimony: Precision Technology
- **Semiconductors**: Used in n-type silicon wafers.
- **Diodes & Detectors**: Infrared detectors and Hall-effect sensors.
- **Solders**: Lead-free solder alloys often contain antimony for durability.

#### Precious Metals
- **Silver & Gold**: Recovered as by-products from Moroccan copper mines, used for non-corroding contact points.
