## Navigating the Commodity Supercycle

The global metal market is undergoing a structural shift driven by the green energy transition and digital revolution. Understanding these macro trends helps buyers make strategic sourcing decisions.

### Key Market Drivers

#### The EV Revolution
Electric vehicles require 6x the mineral inputs of conventional cars. This exponential demand is creating long-term deficits in **Copper**, **Cobalt**, and **Lithium**.
- *Impact*: Prices for battery metals are decoupled from traditional industrial cycles.

#### Supply Chain Diversification
Geopolitical tensions are driving Western nations to diversify supply chains away from single-source dependencies.
- *Morocco's Advantage*: As a stable partner with Free Trade Agreements (FTAs) with both the US and EU, Morocco is a preferred "friend-shoring" destination.

#### Infrastructure Stimulus
Global infrastructure spending (e.g., US Infrastructure Bill, EU Green Deal) underpins steady demand for **Zinc** (galvanization) and **Iron Ore** (steel).
