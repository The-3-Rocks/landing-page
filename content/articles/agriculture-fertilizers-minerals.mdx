## Feeding the Soil

While N-P-K (Nitrogen, Phosphorus, Potassium) are the primary nutrients, crops cannot thrive without essential trace minerals. Morocco's mineral exports play a crucial role in preventing deficiency diseases in plants and animals.

### Vital Micronutrients

#### Zinc Sulfate
- **Crop Health**: Essential for corn, rice, and wheat. Zinc deficiency causes stunted growth and low yields.
- **Animal Feed**: Added to livestock feed to boost immune systems and growth rates.

#### Copper Sulfate
- **Fungicide**: Widely used in vineyards and fruit orchards (Bordeaux mixture) to control fungal infections.
- **Photosynthesis**: A key component in the electron transport chain of plants.

#### Cobalt
- **Nitrogen Fixation**: Required by legumes to fix atmospheric nitrogen.
- **Vitamin B12**: Essential for ruminant animals (cattle, sheep).
