## The Physics of Protection

In nuclear power plants, research labs, and hospitals, protecting personnel from ionizing radiation is a critical engineering challenge. The solution lies in density.

### Shielding Solutions

#### Lead Shielding
- **Efficiency**: Lead's high atomic number makes it the most effective shield against gamma rays and X-rays.
- **Formats**: Lead bricks for temporary walls, lead-lined drywall for X-ray rooms, and lead glass for viewing windows.

#### Barite Concrete
- **Cost-Effective**: For large structures (like bunker walls), mixing barite aggregate into concrete increases density to >3.5 g/cm³ without the cost of pure lead.
- **Structural Integrity**: Provides both load-bearing strength and radiation protection.
