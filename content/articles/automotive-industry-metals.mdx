### Essential Automotive Materials

#### Iron Ore & Steel
- **Chassis & Frame**: High-strength steel from Moroccan iron ore ensures passenger safety.
- **Engine Components**: Cast iron blocks for durability.

#### Zinc: The Guardian Against Rust
- **Galvanization**: Zinc coatings protect steel car bodies from corrosion, extending vehicle life.
- **Die-Casting**: Precision parts for door handles, locks, and safety belt mechanisms.

#### Lead: The Power to Start
- **SLI Batteries**: Starting, Lighting, and Ignition batteries remain standard in nearly all vehicles, including EVs (for auxiliary systems).
- **Recycling**: Lead-acid batteries have a 99% recycling rate, making them a circular economy model.
