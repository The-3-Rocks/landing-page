## The Value of Knowing Your Source

In today's global market, provenance is power. Manufacturers need to know not just what they are buying, but where it came from. **The<span className='text-teal-400'>3</span>Rocks** prioritizes complete transparency from the extraction point to the final delivery.

### Pillars of Transparency

#### 1. Traceability
We implement batch tracking systems that link every shipment of concentrate back to the specific mine shaft and production date. This "Chain of Custody" is documented in our digital logs.

#### 2. Documentation
Every export is accompanied by a comprehensive package:
- **Certificate of Origin**: Stamped by the Moroccan Chamber of Commerce.
- **Certificate of Analysis (CoA)**: Detailing chemical composition.
- **Bill of Lading**: Official shipping record.

#### 3. Ethical Compliance
We adhere to international standards such as the OECD Due Diligence Guidance, ensuring our minerals are conflict-free and extracted without child labor or human rights violations.

### Reducing Risk

Transparent supply chains reduce legal, reputational, and operational risks for our clients. By sourcing directly from reputable Moroccan mines, you eliminate the uncertainty of grey-market aggregators.
