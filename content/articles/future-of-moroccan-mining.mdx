## A New Era of Discovery

Morocco's "Plan Maroc Mines" sets an ambitious agenda to triple the mining sector's turnover by 2030. This strategy focuses on expanding exploration, adding value locally, and modernizing the industry.

### Strategic Pillars

#### 1. Exploration of Cover
Much of Morocco's mineral wealth lies hidden beneath sand or sedimentary cover. New geophysical technologies (aeromagnetics, gravity surveys) are unlocking these concealed deposits in the South.

#### 2. Downstream Processing
Moving up the value chain. Instead of exporting raw ore, the focus is shifting to:
- **Refining**: Producing metal ingots locally.
- **Battery Manufacturing**: Establishing gigafactories to produce EV batteries within Morocco.

#### 3. Strategic Minerals
Prioritizing the "metals of the future": Cobalt, Lithium, Rare Earth Elements (REEs), and Copper.

#### 4. Digital Transformation
Adopting Industry 4.0 standards—automated drilling, IoT monitoring, and digital twins—to increase efficiency and safety.
