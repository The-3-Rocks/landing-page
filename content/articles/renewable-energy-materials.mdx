## The Mineral Foundation of Renewable Energy

The transition to a low-carbon economy is driving unprecedented demand for critical raw materials. Solar panels, wind turbines, and energy storage systems require significantly more minerals than fossil fuel technologies.

### Key Minerals in Green Technology

#### Copper: The Conductive Heart
- **Wind Energy**: A 3MW wind turbine contains up to 4.7 tons of copper.
- **Solar Power**: Photovoltaic systems use approximately 5.5 tons of copper per MW.
- **Grid Infrastructure**: Essential for transmitting renewable energy from remote sources to cities.

#### Cobalt: Energy Storage Stability
- **Grid Storage**: Stabilizing intermittent renewable sources.
- **Battery Life**: Enhancing the longevity and efficiency of storage systems.

#### Lead: Reliable Backup
- **Start-Stop Systems**: Reducing emissions in hybrid vehicles.
- **Backup Power**: Critical for renewable energy grid stabilization.
