### Key Mineral Ingredients

#### Antimony Trioxide
- **Glass Fining**: Removes microscopic bubbles from molten glass, ensuring optical clarity in screens and lenses.
- **Opacifier**: Creates the desirable opaque white finish in sanitaryware and tiles.

#### Barite
- **Glazes**: Improves the brilliance and refractive index of ceramic glazes.
- **Flux**: Lowers the melting point of glass batches, saving energy.
