## Morocco's Mining Regions: A Geological Treasure Trove

Morocco's diverse geology creates distinct mining regions, each specializing in specific minerals. From the **Anti-Atlas mountains** to the **Errachidia province**, Morocco's mineral wealth powers global industries.

### Major Mining Regions

#### 1. Errachidia Province (Lead, Zinc)
**Location**: Southeastern Morocco, Drâa-Tafilalet region

**Key Deposits**:
- **Bni Taddjit Lead-Zinc**: Premier deposit
  - Lead: 86% concentrate, 8,400 tons annually
  - Zinc: +37% ore, 70% concentrate, 50,000+ tons annually
- **Touissit-Bou Beker District**: Historic lead-zinc mining

**Geological Formation**: Paleozoic sedimentary rocks with hydrothermal mineralization

**Infrastructure**:
- Modern processing facilities
- Road access to Casablanca port (550 km)
- Skilled mining workforce

#### 2. Tinghir Region (Copper)
**Location**: High Atlas mountains, south-central Morocco

**Production**:
- Copper ore: 8-21% purity
- Annual extraction: 17,000 tons
- Available stock: 500+ tons

**Mining Heritage**: Copper mining since Phoenician times (3000+ years)

**Geology**: Volcanic-associated massive sulfide deposits

#### 3. Khenifra Region (Antimony)
**Location**: Middle Atlas mountains, central Morocco

**Antimony Deposits**:
- Purity: 30%+ ore
- Production: 25,000 tons annually
- Available: 700+ tons

**Other Minerals**: Minor gold, silver occurrences

**Accessibility**: Central location, good transport links

#### 4. Western Sahara & Southern Provinces (Iron, Barite)
**Iron Ore**:
- Purity: ≥56%
- Production: 120,000 tons annually
- Stock: 40,000 tons

**Barite**:
- Density: 4.18-4.28
- Production: 80,000 tons annually
- Stock: 400 tons

**Strategic Value**: Vast unexplored potential

#### 5. Anti-Atlas Region (Copper, Cobalt)
**Minerals**:
- Copper: High-grade ore bodies
- Cobalt: 42%+ emerging production
- Silver: By-product of copper mining

**Geological Significance**: Precambrian basement rocks, unique mineralization

### Geological Overview

#### Formation History
1. **Precambrian (>540 million years ago)**: Base metal mineralization in Anti-Atlas
2. **Paleozoic (540-250 million years ago)**: Lead-zinc deposits in sedimentary basins
3. **Mesozoic (250-65 million years ago)**: Copper-rich volcanic systems
4. **Cenozoic (\<65 million years ago)**: Uplift and exposure of deposits

#### Mineral Belts
- **Atlas Belt**: Copper, lead, zinc, iron
- **Anti-Atlas Belt**: Copper, cobalt, gold, silver
- **Coastal Belt**: Barite, phosphates
