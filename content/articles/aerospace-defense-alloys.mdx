## Minerals for Extreme Environments

The aerospace and defense sectors demand materials that can withstand extreme heat, pressure, and stress. Moroccan strategic minerals are integral to producing the superalloys and components used in jet engines and defense systems.

### Strategic Aerospace Materials

#### Cobalt: The Heat Shield
- **Superalloys**: Cobalt-based alloys maintain strength at high temperatures (up to 1,100°C), making them essential for jet engine turbines.
- **Reliability**: Critical for safety-critical aerospace components.

#### Copper Alloys
- **Avionics**: High-conductivity wiring for aircraft control systems.
- **Bearings & Bushings**: Beryllium-copper alloys for landing gear components.

#### Antimony
- **Ordnance**: Hardening lead for ammunition reliability.
- **Flame Retardants**: Safety materials for aircraft interiors.
