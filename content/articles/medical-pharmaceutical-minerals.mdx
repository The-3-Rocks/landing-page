### Medical Grade Minerals

#### Barite (Barium Sulfate)
- **Medical Imaging**: Used as a radio-opaque contrast agent for X-rays and CT scans of the gastrointestinal tract.
- **Radiation Protection**: High-density barite concrete shields patients and staff in radiology rooms.

#### Zinc Compounds
- **Dermatology**: Calamine (zinc oxide + iron oxide) is a staple for treating skin irritation (Morocco produces premium calamine).
- **Pharmaceuticals**: Essential nutrient supplements and wound healing formulations.

#### Copper: The Natural Sanitizer
- **Antimicrobial Surfaces**: Copper alloys kill 99.9% of bacteria within two hours, reducing hospital-acquired infections (HAIs) on touch surfaces like bed rails and door handles.
