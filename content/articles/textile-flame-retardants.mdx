Flame retardants are invisible heroes. Incorporated into furniture, electronics, and automotive interiors, they slow the spread of fire, providing critical escape time. Morocco is a leading supplier of the key ingredient: Antimony.

### Antimony Trioxide (ATO)

#### Synergistic Effect
Antimony isn't a flame retardant on its own. However, when combined with halogens (in plastics or coatings), it acts as a powerful synergist.
- **Mechanism**: It interferes with the combustion chemistry in the gas phase, scavenging the free radicals that sustain the fire.

#### Applications
- **Textiles**: Upholstery, curtains, and workwear treated with back-coatings.
- **Plastics**: PVC cables, electronics housings, and automotive dashboards.
