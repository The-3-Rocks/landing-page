## Raw Materials for Battery Production: Powering the Energy Revolution

The global transition to electric vehicles and renewable energy demands unprecedented volumes of battery raw materials. Morocco supplies critical minerals including **lead** (for lead-acid batteries), **cobalt** (for lithium-ion batteries), and **antimony** (for battery grids and flame retardants).

### Lead-Acid Batteries

#### Applications
- **Automotive Starting Batteries**: 12V SLI (Starting, Lighting, Ignition)
- **Industrial Backup Power**: UPS systems, telecommunications
- **Energy Storage**: Grid-scale renewable energy storage
- **Forklift & Material Handling**: Deep-cycle batteries

#### Moroccan Lead for Batteries
- **Purity**: 86% concentrate, 99.97% refined
- **Annual Production**: 8,400 tons
- **Forms**: Concentrate, powder, ingots
- **Recyclability**: 99% of lead-acid batteries recycled

### Lithium-Ion Battery Materials

#### Cobalt's Critical Role
- **Cathode Material**: NMC (Nickel-Manganese-Cobalt) batteries
- **Energy Density**: Increases battery capacity
- **Stability**: Prevents thermal runaway
- **EV Demand**: Growing 15-20% annually

#### Moroccan Cobalt
- **Purity**: 42%+ ore, 99.8% refined
- **Ethical Sourcing**: Transparent supply chain
- **Applications**: Electric vehicles, consumer electronics, power tools

### Antimony in Batteries

#### Functions
- **Lead-Acid Battery Grids**: Hardening agent (3-6% antimony)
- **Flame Retardants**: Battery pack safety
- **Alloy Production**: Improved mechanical properties

#### Moroccan Antimony Specifications
- **Purity**: 30%+ ore, 99.65% refined
- **Production**: 25,000 tons annually
