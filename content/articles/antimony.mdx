Antimony (Sb) is a metalloid with important applications in metallurgy, flame retardants, electronics, and batteries. Its unique properties contribute to improved material performance and safety.

### 1. Geological Context

Antimony is primarily found in the sulfide mineral stibnite (Sb₂S₃). Deposits are formed via hydrothermal processes, often in association with lead, silver, and gold ores.

Morocco’s Central Hercynian Massif hosts significant antimony-bearing deposits.

### 2. Properties of Antimony

- **Brittle Metalloid**: Used to strengthen alloys.
- **High Melting Point (631°C)**: Suitable for high-temperature applications.
- **Flame Retardant**: Antimony trioxide enhances fire resistance.
- **Semiconductor Uses**: Important in diodes and infrared detectors.

### 3. Industrial Applications

#### Metallurgy

- **Alloy Hardening**: Antimony improves hardness and mechanical strength in lead and other metal alloys.
- **Battery Production**: Used in lead-acid battery grids to enhance performance.

#### Flame Retardants

- **Fire Safety**: Antimony compounds are incorporated into plastics, textiles, and electronics to reduce flammability.

#### Electronics and Semiconductors

- **Diodes and Infrared Devices**: Key component in semiconductor manufacturing.
